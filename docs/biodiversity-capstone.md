## Learning artifact

`tutorials/DIMER_Philippine_Biodiversity_Field_Survey_Capstone.ipynb` is generated by
`tools/build_biodiversity_capstone.py`. It carries the runtime, CPU contracts, unchanged
pipeline module, model manifests, frozen data manifest, exclusions and a fully hashed
Python 3.12 dependency lock. No repository clone or DIMER worker is required. The default
run uses a T4 and stages BioCLIP 2 and SigLIP 2 sequentially. Model downloads total about
3.25 GB; allow at least 12 GiB free disk for the isolated environment and artifacts.

The frozen sample uses four Philippine species, 14 photos each: 8 train, 3 validation,
3 test. Each photographer belongs to only one role globally, and each class has at least
two photographers in each role. Photo-level CC0/CC BY attribution is retained; precise
locations are excluded. Source photos and photo-derived figures stay out of the results
ZIP. The notebook includes two other-bird, two non-bird and one blank challenge probes.
Small subjects, poor lighting and obstructing foliage are retained as field-data limitations.
Visual composition review is not expert taxonomic verification.

## Scientific controls

- Eight systems: majority, RGB colour centroid, BioCLIP 5-NN, common/scientific-name
  zero-shot prompts for each of two models, and a BioCLIP head.
- The head starts from scientific-text weights; only its weights and bias train. Epoch
  zero and 20 epochs compete by minimum validation cross-entropy, with earlier ties.
- The validation-selected margin policy targets 80% selective accuracy at 50% minimum
  coverage. If infeasible, its stated fallback remains visible. Test results are unfiltered
  for the primary comparison. A display-only threshold activity cannot change that policy.
- Accuracy, fixed-vocabulary macro-F1, top-2, per-class and confusion results accompany
  both record-bootstrap and observer-cluster-bootstrap intervals. Tiny samples and
  unresolved model-pretraining overlap limit interpretation.
- Fresh-process reload reconstructs image features and checks scores, top-1/top-2 and
  referral decisions. Per-stage source/config/product hashes refuse stale prerequisites.
- Final CSV metrics are recomputed from exported predictions; archive CRCs and member
  digests are checked. Completion notes are optional, not required submissions.

## Resolved specification details

1. Taxon matching includes verified subspecies whose recorded ancestor is exactly the
   target species. The observed taxon and lineage remain in the manifest; this preserves
   the original four species without accepting genus-level ambiguity.
2. Joint integer assignment maximises represented class/observer/role units before
   minimum photo hash-rank sum. This replaces role-first truncation, which could violate
   the two-photographer requirement. Equal-objective solver ties are not claimed to be
   lexicographically optimal. The frozen output, not a live query, defines learner data.
3. Early comparison panels show validation results. Test metrics appear only after the
   head epoch and referral policy are locked, resolving contradictory evaluation ordering.
4. Grouped BYOD requires at least six observers per class and a feasible whole-observer
   split, because two observers in each of three roles cannot be achieved with three.
   Missing observer groups permits inference only, not adaptation or evaluation claims.
5. The capstone head uses the explicit `dimer-birds-linear-head-v1` manifest and its own
   strict loader. It is not interchangeable with the original pipeline's adapter format.
6. The isolated CUDA dependency environment needs more than the proposed 5 GB allowance;
   12 GiB is the initial preflight minimum, still subject to hosted measurement.
